Re: A trusted companion for your patients’ medical appointments and follow-up care.

Thank you for the opportunity to introduce SeniorNavigator Services. We are a Mississauga-based, non-medical senior support service dedicated to one simple promise — that no older adult should have to face a clinic corridor, a check-in kiosk, or a confusing set of follow-up instructions alone.

Our trained companions accompany seniors to medical appointments, assist with hospital check-in and paperwork, help interpret after-visit instructions, and provide dependable support with pharmacy and grocery errands. Every visit is delivered with patience, cultural sensitivity, and multilingual care in English, Urdu, and Pashto — and families receive a clear update after each one.

We would welcome the chance to leave brochures with your outpatient team and to arrange a brief introductory session at your convenience. Please feel free to reach me directly at the number above.
